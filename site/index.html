His Story

Raised in the champions' gym

Sahil Gujjar has been boxing since he was 8 years old — nearly two decades inside elite boxing circles, the kind of rooms where world champions shadowbox beside you and every round is a lesson.

He trained at top-tier gyms alongside world-class fighters and coaches: world title challenger Egis Kavaliauskas, former world champion Evgeniy Gradovich and coach Marcos Contreras. These are the same training environments and conditioning philosophies that produced legends like Vasiliy Lomachenko.

Inside those gyms, Sahil learned that real fighting shape isn't about size. It's fluid movement, mental endurance, agility and knowing how to manage your energy for every round. Today he brings those exact principles out of the fight gym and into the lives of busy men and professionals — people who don't need a title shot, but do want to feel sharp, light on their feet and capable again.

“I take those high-level conditioning principles — fluid movement, mental endurance, agility and energy management — and tailor them for busy men who want their competitive physical edge back.”

— Sahil Gujjar

Training Philosophy

Five rounds. One standard.

Every routine, every check-in and every piece of advice comes back to five principles.

  1. Round1

    Real vitality over artificial bulk

    No more afternoon crash, brain fog, bloating or constant fatigue. Training isn't about vanity — it's about waking up sharp, feeling light on your feet and keeping explosive energy all day.

  2. Round2

    Functional capability & fluidity

    Bodybuilding leaves you stiff and calisthenics can feel rigid. Boxing conditioning builds a body like armor — fluid, agile, quick and genuinely useful, with real reflexes, speed and endurance.

  3. Round3

    Deep internal confidence

    True confidence comes from knowing what your body can do. Boxing gives you poise, real-world sharpness and the peace of mind that you can protect yourself and your family.

  4. Round4

    A sport for life & family legacy

    Not a 30-day fad. Boxing is a master discipline — the habits, physical skill and mental toughness you build here are something you can pass down to your kids.

  5. Round5

    Authentic, accessible excellence

    No overpriced gimmicks. Championship-level conditioning concepts, delivered directly to everyday men and busy professionals for $100 a month.
